What to do while we are on the way

  1. Keep doors closed. A closed cool room holds its temperature far longer than one being opened every few minutes.
  2. Check the display and write down the temperature and the time. It helps us diagnose the fault and helps your food safety records.
  3. Move high-value and high-risk stock first: meat, seafood, dairy, cream, cooked food and anything pre-prepared.
  4. Use a working fridge, esky with ice or a neighbouring unit for the stock you move.
  5. Do not keep switching the unit off and on. Repeated restarts can damage a compressor.
  6. Note the brand, model number and any fault code. You will find the model number on the data plate.

How long can food stay out of the fridge?

In NSW, potentially hazardous food such as meat, dairy, cooked rice and prepared salads should be kept at 5°C or below. The general food safety guide is that food held between 5°C and 60°C for less than 2 hours can go back in the fridge or be used, between 2 and 4 hours it should be used straight away, and beyond 4 hours it should be thrown out. If you are not sure, check with your local council environmental health officer or the NSW Food Authority.

Commercial Fridge Repair Cost in Blacktown

We do not believe in vague pricing. The call-out fee starts from $69, and you get an upfront quote before any work starts. The final repair price depends on the fault, so we confirm it on site before we start work. These are the things that move the price:

  • The part that has failed. A door seal or fan motor is a very different job to a compressor.
  • Whether the unit needs refrigerant, and which type.
  • The age and model of the unit, and how easy it is to get parts.
  • Access. A unit in a tight kitchen takes longer than one on an open floor.
  • Time of call. After-hours work is quoted before we attend, so you know the price first.

Repair or replace?

As a rough guide, a repair makes sense when the unit is under about 8 to 10 years old and the fault is a common part such as a seal, fan, controller or probe. Replacement starts to make more sense when a unit is older, needs a major component like a compressor and also uses a gas that is getting hard to source. We will tell you honestly if a repair is not worth it. We would rather you spend your money wisely and book us next time.

Commercial Refrigeration Maintenance and Servicing

Most commercial breakdowns can be traced back to something that was left alone for too long. A regular commercial refrigeration service finds these problems early, when they are cheap to fix.

A maintenance visit can include:

  • Cleaning the condenser coils
  • Checking and cleaning the evaporator and drain
  • Testing fans and defrost
  • Checking door seals and hinges
  • Testing the thermostat and temperature accuracy
  • Checking electrical connections
  • Looking at refrigerant pressures

For busy kitchens we suggest a service every 6 months. For cafes and smaller shops, once a year is a good start. Ask us about a maintenance visit that suits your equipment.
