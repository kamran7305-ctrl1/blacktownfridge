Does Your Fridge Need a Regas? Warning Signs

A fridge that has lost refrigerant tends to fade rather than fail overnight. Look for these:

Fridge and Freezer Both Warm

The compressor runs but neither section gets properly cold.

Compressor Running Non-Stop

It never cycles off and the fridge never reaches temperature.

Hot Compressor or Condenser

The back or bottom of the fridge is hotter than usual.

Frost Only at the Start of the Coil

The freezer frosts near the inlet and nowhere else.

Oily Residue at a Joint or Pipe

Refrigerant oil leaks out with the gas.

Slowly Getting Worse

Cooling fades over weeks or months, not overnight.

Hissing Sound

A faint hiss when the compressor starts.

Rising Power Bills

The fridge works harder to do less.

Cooling Fault You Can't Explain

Fans, seals and thermostat all test fine.

These signs overlap with fan, defrost and control board faults, so a test comes first. See our fridge not cooling repair page for the other causes.

Fridge Gas Refill: What Regas Actually Means

Your fridge cools with a sealed circuit of refrigerant that is compressed, cooled in the condenser, expanded and then absorbs heat in the evaporator. The gas is not consumed. It goes round and round for the life of the fridge. If the circuit ends up short of gas, the gas has escaped through a leak, and the leak is the real fault.

That is why a fridge gas refill on its own rarely lasts. A proper fridge regas has three parts: find and repair the leak, evacuate the system to remove air and moisture, and recharge it with the correct refrigerant, weighed to the amount on the rating plate. Too little gas cools poorly. Too much can damage the compressor.

It is also why we don't sell DIY regas kits. Most modern household fridges use a flammable hydrocarbon refrigerant in a very small charge, and the wrong gas, the wrong amount or air left in the system will not give you a working fridge.

Fridge Gas Leak Repair: Where Leaks Happen

Gas leaks have a few usual hiding places. Knowing where yours is decides whether the fridge is worth repairing.

  • Pipe joints and solder points: often repairable, and the cheapest leaks to fix.
  • Condenser coil: corrosion, damage from a knock or a rubbing pipe. Can usually be repaired or replaced.
  • Evaporator coil: corrosion from moisture and cleaning chemicals, or a nick from chipping ice. Harder to reach.
  • Compressor connections and service valves: vibration can crack a joint over time.
  • Pipes in the door or around a dispenser: chafing wear on fridges with built-in heaters or water lines.

Fridge Evaporator Repair

The evaporator is the cold coil, usually behind the freezer back panel. A leak here is the one we dread, because many evaporators are sealed behind the liner. We check whether the coil can be reached and repaired, whether a replacement coil is available and whether it makes sense for the age of the fridge. Many evaporator faults that look like a leak are actually a defrost problem with ice choking the coil, which is a much smaller repair, so that is the first thing we rule out.

Fridge Condenser Repair

The condenser is the coil at the back or underneath that gets rid of heat. It leaks less often than the evaporator, but it clogs with dust and pet hair, and a dirty condenser makes a fridge run hot and constantly, which looks like a gas problem. A clean often fixes it. A leaking condenser can be repaired or replaced, and a failed condenser fan motor is replaced separately.

Fridge Regas FAQs

What does fridge regas mean?

Regas means recharging the sealed cooling system with the correct refrigerant. A fridge does not use up its gas like a car uses fuel, so if the system is low, there is a leak. A proper fridge regas finds and repairs the leak first, removes air and moisture from the system, then recharges it to the weight on the rating plate.

Can I just top up my fridge gas?

A top-up without a leak repair is only a temporary fix. The gas escapes again, usually within weeks, and you pay twice. Many modern fridges also use a flammable hydrocarbon refrigerant, so topping up with a DIY kit is unsafe. We find the leak, repair it and then recharge.

How much does fridge regas cost in Blacktown?

Our call-out fee starts from $69 and it covers the visit and the diagnosis. The price of the regas depends on the leak location, the repair needed, the refrigerant type and the age of the fridge. We confirm an upfront quote with you before any sealed-system work begins. Seniors receive a discount.

How do I know if my fridge needs a gas refill?

Typical signs are a fridge and freezer that are both warm while the compressor runs, a freezer that frosts only at the start of the evaporator, a compressor that runs constantly, a hot compressor or condenser, and sometimes an oily residue at a joint or pipe. These signs overlap with fan, defrost and board faults, so a technician needs to test before calling it a gas problem.

Is it worth regassing an old fridge?

If the fridge is under about 10 years old and the leak is at an accessible joint, pipe or condenser, repair and regas can be worth it. If the leak is inside a corroded evaporator in an old fridge, replacement is often the better call. We will tell you honestly before you spend money.

What refrigerant does my fridge use?

It is printed on the rating plate, usually inside the fridge or on the back. Older household fridges commonly use R134a. Most newer ones use R600a (isobutane), and some use R290 (propane). The right type and charge weight matter, and mixing gases can damage the system.

Can you repair a fridge evaporator or condenser?

Often, yes. A leaking condenser or a joint in the pipework can usually be repaired or replaced. A leaking evaporator inside the cabinet is harder, because many are sealed behind the liner. We assess it and tell you whether a repair is practical.

Can you regas a freezer or a bar fridge?

Yes. The same rule applies: we find the leak, repair it and then recharge. Small bar fridges and old chest freezers are often not worth a sealed-system repair, and we will say so.

Is my fridge not cooling because it needs gas?

Not always. A failed fan, a defrost fault, a bad sensor or a dead start relay can look the same. We test the whole system first so you don't pay for a regas that doesn't fix it. See our fridge not cooling repair page for the other causes.

How long does a fridge regas take?

A leak repair and recharge usually takes a few hours, because the system has to be repaired, evacuated, charged and then tested. If a part has to be ordered we tell you how long it will take and how to keep your food safe in the meantime.

Can you regas my fridge on the same day?

Often, yes, if the leak is found and the parts are on the van. Same-day appointments are available in many cases, depending on when you contact us and where you are.

Does a fridge regas come with a warranty?

Yes. Every repair has a parts and labour warranty of up to 12 months.
