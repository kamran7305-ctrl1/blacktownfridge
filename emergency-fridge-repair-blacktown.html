Urgent Fridge & Freezer Faults We Fix

These are the breakdowns we're called out for most often. Describe the symptom when you book and we'll come prepared.

Fridge not cooling

Often a dirty condenser, a failed evaporator fan, a faulty start relay or low refrigerant. The unit sounds like it's running but the compartment is warm.

Freezer not freezing

Usually a defrost fault, blocked airflow or a struggling compressor. Frozen food starts to soften, so speed matters.

Compressor not starting

Repeated clicking, humming without cooling or constant running can point to a relay, overload or compressor fault that needs testing.

Water leaking

Typically a blocked defrost drain or a faulty ice-maker line. Water pooling on the floor is best dealt with before it damages flooring.

Power trips or burning smell

Switch the unit off at the wall and call us. Electrical faults, failed wiring or seized motors should never be left running.

Coolroom failure

A coolroom that can't hold temperature puts thousands of dollars of stock at risk overnight. We respond fast to protect it.

Fan motor failure

A dead evaporator or condenser fan stops cold air circulating, causing warm spots and rapid temperature rise.

Error codes and display faults

Modern fridges flash codes for sensor, fan, defrost and communication errors. We read and interpret them properly rather than guessing.

What to Do While You Wait for Us

A few simple steps can save your food and make the repair faster.

1

Keep the doors closed

Every opening lets cold air out. As a rough guide, a closed fridge holds its temperature for around four hours, and a full, closed freezer for roughly 24 to 48 hours.

2

Check the basics

Confirm the unit has power and the temperature controls haven't been bumped. Avoid repeatedly switching it on and off.

3

Protect your food

Move essentials such as milk, meat and medication into an esky with ice. If in doubt about anything that has warmed up, throw it out.

4

Smell burning? Switch it off

If you notice a burning smell, sparks or the power keeps tripping, turn the fridge off at the wall and let us know when you book.

5

Note the details

The brand, model, any error code on the display and what you noticed first all help us diagnose faster.

6

Send your request

Use the form at the top of this page. Booking is open 24/7, so you can lock in a time even at midnight.

Emergency Fridge Repair FAQs

Do you offer emergency fridge repair in Blacktown?

Yes. We take emergency and after-hours fridge repair requests across Blacktown 24/7, including weekends and public holidays. Same-day call-outs may be available depending on the time of your request, technician availability and your location.

How do I book an emergency fridge repair?

Fill in the online request form on this page at any hour and tell us what has happened. We give you a free quote over the phone before any work begins, and a technician is arranged for the earliest available time.

What should I do while I wait for the technician?

Keep the fridge and freezer doors closed to hold the cold in, check the unit has power and that the temperature controls haven't been changed, and avoid repeatedly switching it on and off. If you can smell burning or see sparks, switch the unit off at the wall and tell us when you book.

How long does food stay safe in a fridge that has stopped working?

As a rough guide, an unopened fridge keeps food cold for around four hours, and a full, unopened freezer for roughly 24 to 48 hours. Perishable food that has been above 5°C for more than a couple of hours should be treated with caution. When in doubt, throw it out.

Do you handle emergency repairs for commercial fridges and coolrooms?

Yes. We repair commercial fridges, freezers, display units and coolrooms for cafes, restaurants, shops, clinics and other businesses. If stock or trading is at risk, mention it when you book so we can prioritise accordingly.

Is it worth repairing my fridge or should I replace it?

It depends on the age of the unit, the fault and the repair cost. Every job starts with a diagnosis, and we will tell you plainly if repair isn't good value, before you pay for any work.

Is there a warranty on emergency repairs?

Yes. Repairs are backed by our 1-year workmanship and parts warranty, the same as our standard bookings.
