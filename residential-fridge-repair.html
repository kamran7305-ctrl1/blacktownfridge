Is Your Home Fridge Faulty? Common Warning Signs

Most household fridges tell you something is wrong before they stop completely. Get in touch if you notice any of these:

Fridge Isn't Cold Enough

Milk goes off early or food feels warm. A fridge should sit at 5°C or below.

Food Freezing in the Fridge

Lettuce and drinks freeze at the back. Usually a thermostat or sensor fault.

Water Inside or on the Floor

Often a blocked defrost drain or a split water line.

New or Loud Noises

Buzzing, clicking, rattling or a fan that squeals.

Running Non-Stop

The compressor never cycles off but the fridge stays warm.

Door Won't Seal

A torn or hardened gasket lets warm air in and frost builds up.

Frost Behind the Back Panel

A defrost heater or sensor has failed and ice blocks airflow.

Error Code or Flashing Light

Control board, sensor or fan fault on a digital fridge.

Rising Power Bills

An older or struggling fridge uses more electricity.

If the fridge has already warmed up and the food is at risk, see our emergency fridge repair service.

Home Fridge Repairs: Every Type We Fix

A household fridge is not one machine. A built-in unit behind a cabinet door fails differently to a double door fridge or a camping fridge on a 12V supply. That is why we ask what type you have when you book.

Built-In and Integrated Fridge Repair

Built-in fridge repair and integrated fridge repair are the jobs people worry about most, because the fridge is part of the kitchen. An integrated fridge sits behind a cabinet-matched door, and a built-in fridge is fitted into joinery with fixed ventilation around it. The cabinetry usually gets in the way, and a careless removal can chip a panel or a benchtop. We repair these in place wherever we can. Common faults include:

  • Evaporator and condenser fans that have failed or are noisy
  • Temperature sensors and control boards
  • Door hinges, panels and seals that have dropped out of alignment
  • Defrost heaters and blocked drains
  • Overheating caused by poor ventilation around the unit
  • Ice makers, water filters and water valves

If a built-in fridge has to be slid out, we tell you first, protect the floor and the surrounding cabinets, and refit it so the door lines up and closes properly.

Double Door Fridge Repair

Double door fridge repair covers French door, side-by-side and twin-door fridges. These models carry more fans, sensors, dampers and water lines than a single-door fridge, so there are more places for a fault to hide. We diagnose both compartments together and repair:

  • A warm fridge side with a freezer that is still cold
  • Frost and ice behind the back panels
  • Ice makers and water dispensers that stop working
  • Water leaking under the doors or onto the floor
  • Door seals, hinges and uneven closing
  • Control boards, display errors and sensors

Fridge Freezer Repair

Fridge freezer repair is the most common home job we do. In a frost-free fridge freezer, one fan feeds cold air to both sections, so a problem in the freezer often shows up in the fridge, and the other way round. Top-mount, bottom-mount and bar fridge freezers all follow this pattern. See our freezer repair page if the main problem is the freezer side.

Kitchen, Garage and Second Fridges

Kitchen fridge repair is usually about airflow, seals and dirty coils. A second fridge in the garage or on the back verandah works much harder in a Western Sydney summer, and it often runs for years with a seal that no longer closes. If it is struggling, an inexpensive repair can save you an expensive replacement.

Camping Fridge Repair

A camping fridge fails for different reasons. Most are 12V or 12/240V compressor fridges that run from a car battery or a portable power station. If yours cuts out, won't start, trips the low-voltage protection, runs loudly or has stopped cooling before a trip, we can test the power supply, wiring and plug, thermostat, fan and compressor. Some small coolers are not worth repairing, and we will say so.

Why Is My Fridge Not Cooling?

These are the causes we find most often in household fridges, from the simplest to the most serious:

  1. Temperature dial or setting changed. A bumped dial or a digital panel in the wrong mode is the simplest cause.
  2. Blocked vents or overpacked shelves. Food pressed against the back wall or air vents stops cold air circulating.
  3. Worn door seal. Warm air leaks in and the fridge works harder.
  4. Dirty condenser coils. Dust and pet hair make the fridge run hot.
  5. Failed fan motor. The evaporator fan moves cold air from the freezer into the fridge. If it stops, the fridge warms up.
  6. Defrost system fault. Ice blocks the evaporator and airflow drops.
  7. Faulty thermostat, sensor or control board. The fridge stops cooling at the wrong temperature or gives an error.
  8. Compressor or start relay failure. The motor hums, clicks or will not run.
  9. Refrigerant leak. The fridge runs but never gets cold enough.

Causes 1 to 4 are quick checks you can do yourself. For anything else, please don't take the fridge apart. Sealed-system work and live electrical parts need a trained, licensed technician.

Quick Checks Before You Call

These simple steps can save you a service fee, or at least help us diagnose faster:

  • Check the fridge is plugged in and the power point works
  • Check the safety switch or circuit breaker hasn't tripped
  • Look at the temperature setting. It should give about 3°C to 5°C in the fridge
  • Pull back anything blocking the air vents
  • Check the door seal closes on a sheet of paper
  • For a built-in fridge, make sure the ventilation gaps around it are clear
  • Note any noises, error codes and when the problem began
  • Keep the doors closed to protect the food

Still not cold? Email us and describe what you've found.

What To Do With Your Food While the Fridge Is Down

Keep the doors shut. A closed fridge holds its cold for a few hours. Move the highest-risk food first: meat, seafood, dairy, cooked leftovers, baby milk and medication. Put it in a working freezer, or in an esky with ice or frozen packs.

As a general food safety guide, cold food that has been above 5°C for less than 2 hours can go back in the fridge or be used. Between 2 and 4 hours, use it straight away. After 4 hours, throw it out. When in doubt, throw it out. For official advice, see the NSW Food Authority.

Residential Fridge Repair Cost: Repair or Replace?

Our call-out fee is $69, and it covers the visit and the diagnosis. The repair price depends on the fault. A door seal or a drain is a smaller job, a fan motor or defrost part sits in the middle, and a compressor or a sealed-system leak is the biggest. We confirm the price with you before we start. What affects it: the part that failed, the age and model, whether refrigerant is needed, and how easy the fridge is to reach, which matters for built-in units.

  • Repair if the fridge is under about 10 years old and the fault is a fan, seal, sensor, thermostat or defrost part.
  • Consider replacing if it is very old and the compressor has failed or the sealed system is leaking.
  • Replace if the cabinet is rusted, the insulation is wet or the repair costs more than about half the price of a comparable new fridge.
  • Check running costs. An old fridge can use far more electricity than a modern energy-rated one.

We give you honest advice on the spot. If a repair isn't worth it, we'll say so.

Keep Your Home Fridge Running Longer

  • Keep the fridge at 3°C to 5°C and the freezer at -18°C
  • Leave gaps around the fridge for air to move, and never block the ventilation around a built-in unit
  • Clean the condenser coils once or twice a year
  • Wipe the door seals and check they grip a sheet of paper
  • Don't overfill the shelves or press food against the back wall
  • Let hot food cool before it goes in
  • Keep the fridge out of direct sun and away from the oven
  • Fix small noises, frost or leaks early

Residential Fridge Repair FAQs

How much does residential fridge repair cost?

Our call-out fee is $69. The repair price depends on what has failed, the age and model of the fridge, and how easy it is to reach, so we diagnose first and give you an upfront quote before any work starts. Seniors receive a discount.

Do you repair built-in and integrated fridges?

Yes. Built-in and integrated fridges sit behind cabinetry, so we work carefully to avoid damaging joinery and door panels. Most faults, such as fans, sensors, seals, defrost parts and control boards, are fixed in place without pulling the fridge out.

Can you repair a double door fridge?

Yes. That includes French door, side-by-side and twin-door fridges. They have more fans, sensors and defrost parts than a single-door fridge, which is why a proper diagnosis matters before parts are replaced.

Do you repair camping fridges?

Yes, for most portable compressor fridges and 12V camping fridges. Common faults are power or voltage cut-out problems, wiring and plug issues, fans, thermostats and sealed-system faults. Three-way and thermoelectric coolers are assessed case by case, because some are not worth repairing.

My fridge is warm but the freezer works. What is wrong?

Usually the fridge is not getting enough cold air. It can be a blocked air vent, a failed evaporator fan, a defrost fault or a damper that has stuck. It is a common household fridge fault and often fixable in one visit.

Why is my fridge leaking water inside?

Water at the bottom of the fridge is most often a blocked or frozen defrost drain. Water on the floor can also come from a split water line to an ice maker or dispenser. Both are simple repairs for a technician.

Is it worth repairing an old fridge?

If the fridge is under about 10 years old and the fault is a fan, seal, sensor, thermostat or defrost part, repair usually makes sense. If a very old fridge has a failed compressor or a leaking sealed system, replacement is often the better call. We will tell you honestly.

How long does a home fridge repair take?

Most visits take one to two hours. If we carry the part, the fridge is usually fixed on the first visit. If a part has to be ordered, we tell you how long it will take and how to keep your food safe in the meantime.

Can I get a same-day fridge repair at home?

Often, yes. Same-day appointments are available in many cases, depending on the time you contact us and where you are. Send us the details early and we will tell you what is possible.

Do you repair fridges across Sydney or only Blacktown?

We are based in Blacktown and service Western Sydney, including Parramatta, Penrith, Castle Hill and the north-west. If you are further out, contact us and we will confirm whether we can reach you.

Does residential fridge repair come with a warranty?

Yes. Every repair carries a 1 year parts and labour warranty.

Do you repair freezers and commercial fridges as well?

Yes. See our freezer repair and commercial fridge repair pages. If your fridge has failed after hours with food inside, see our emergency fridge repair service.
