Common Refrigerator & Freezer Problems

Recognise the symptom and you'll know what to tell us when you book. Here's what each common fault usually means.

Fridge not cooling

Often a dirty condenser, a failed evaporator fan, a faulty start relay or low refrigerant. The compartment feels warm even though the unit sounds like it's running.

Freezer not freezing

Usually a defrost fault, blocked airflow or a struggling compressor. Ice cream softens and frozen goods start to sweat.

Ice build-up

A defrost heater, timer or sensor fault — or a worn door seal letting humid air in that freezes onto the coils.

Water leaking

Typically a blocked defrost drain or a faulty ice-maker line. Water pools under the crisper or on the floor. A quick, cheap fix when caught early.

Loud or unusual noises

Rattling or buzzing points to a failing condenser or evaporator fan. Loud clicking often means a struggling start relay or compressor.

Compressor always running

Dirty coils, a leaking door seal or low gas force the compressor to run non-stop. You'll notice extra heat around the unit and higher power bills.

Fridge warm but freezer cold

A classic airflow or damper fault — the freezer works, but cold air isn't reaching the fridge section. Very common on frost-free models.

Thermostat faults

A failed thermostat or temperature sensor leaves the fridge too warm, or freezes your food unpredictably.

Error codes

Modern fridges flash fault codes for sensor, fan, defrost and communication errors. We read and interpret them properly rather than guessing.

Fan motor failure

A dead evaporator or condenser fan stops cold air circulating, causing uneven cooling and warm spots.

Door seal problems

Perished or torn gaskets let cold escape, driving up running costs and causing sweating and ice. A cheap repair with a big payoff.

Display panel issues

Unresponsive or glitching control panels and touch displays — often a board or connection fault we can test and repair rather than replace the whole unit.

Repair vs Replace Guide

We'd rather give you the truth than sell you a repair you'll regret. Here's the rule of thumb we use when we diagnose your fridge.

Situation Usually worth repairing?
Fridge under ~8 years old with a common part fault (fan, thermostat, seal, relay) Yes — a straightforward, cost-effective fix.
Mid-range fridge, repair cost under roughly half the replacement price Usually — especially with a warranty on the work.
Premium French-door or built-in unit Often — replacement is expensive, so repair frequently makes sense.
Older budget fridge (10+ years) with a failed compressor or sealed-system leak Often not — the fix approaches the cost of a new unit.
An ageing unit with repeated, recurring faults Consider replacing — we'll give you a candid view.

Age, the specific fault, energy efficiency and the repair cost against a new unit all matter. When we assess your fridge, we'll tell you plainly which way we'd go if it were ours — and we only recommend replacement when it's genuinely the better value.

Preventative Maintenance Tips

A little upkeep goes a long way in Blacktown's climate — especially heading into summer.

Residential fridges & freezers

Vacuum the condenser coils a couple of times a year, keep the unit a few centimetres off the wall for airflow, check door seals for cracks and gaps, and don't overpack — air needs to circulate to cool evenly.

Commercial refrigeration

Schedule regular coil cleaning and gasket checks, keep condensers clear of kitchen grease, and log temperatures daily so a slow drift is caught before it becomes a breakdown.

Coolrooms

Inspect door seals and closers, keep evaporators clear of ice, and book periodic servicing — a coolroom failure is far cheaper to prevent than to recover from.

Display fridges

Clean glass, fans and drains regularly, and never block internal vents with stock — restricted airflow is the number one reason display units lose temperature.

Freezers

Defrost before ice gets thick, check the seal, and keep the unit out of direct heat and sun where you can.

FAQs

How quickly can you repair my fridge in Blacktown?

Same-day appointments may be available depending on the time of your booking, technician availability, your location and the type of fault. Contact us as early as possible if the refrigerator has completely stopped cooling.

What should I do if my fridge suddenly stops cooling?

Keep the doors closed as much as possible to retain cold air. Check that the refrigerator has power and confirm that the temperature controls have not been accidentally changed. If the fridge remains warm, arrange professional diagnosis rather than repeatedly switching it on and off.

Do you repair both the fridge and freezer sections?

Yes, applicable faults can be investigated in both refrigerator and freezer compartments. Sometimes a freezer may remain cold while the fridge becomes warm because of an airflow or defrost problem.

What refrigerator brands do you repair?

We service many commonly used brands, including Samsung, LG, Westinghouse, Fisher & Paykel, Electrolux, Bosch, Haier and Hisense, subject to model, fault and parts availability.

Is it worth repairing an old refrigerator?

It depends on the refrigerator's age, condition, efficiency, fault and repair cost. A relatively minor fault can still make an older fridge worth repairing, while major sealed-system or compressor repairs on some older appliances may not be economical. A diagnosis should come before the decision.

Why is my freezer cold but my fridge warm?

Possible causes include an evaporator fan problem, blocked airflow, damper fault, excessive ice caused by a defrost problem or another cooling-system issue. The exact cause requires diagnosis.

Why is my fridge leaking water?

Common causes include blocked defrost drains, water-line problems, condensation, drain-pan issues and poor door sealing.

Can you repair commercial refrigerators in Blacktown?

Yes, applicable commercial refrigerators and refrigeration equipment can be inspected and repaired. If the breakdown is affecting business operations or stored products, mention this when booking.

Do you provide emergency fridge repairs?

Urgent and same-day appointments may be available. Contact us directly to confirm the earliest available technician.

How much does a fridge repair cost?

Repair costs vary according to the appliance, fault, labour and parts required. The best approach is to diagnose the problem before deciding whether the repair is worthwhile.
