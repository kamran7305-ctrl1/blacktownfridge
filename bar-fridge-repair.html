Signs Your Bar Fridge Needs Repair

Compact fridges fail in the same ways as bigger ones. Get in touch if you notice any of these:

Not Cold Enough

Drinks or food stay warm even on the coldest setting.

Freezing Everything

Food or drinks freeze, or ice builds up inside.

Loud Noises

Rattling, humming, clicking or a fan that squeals.

Water Leaking

Water inside the fridge or on the floor.

Door Will Not Seal

A torn seal, or a door that hangs open.

Runs Non-Stop

The compressor never switches off.

Light Stays On or Off

A faulty door switch or light.

Trips the Power

The fridge will not start or trips the breaker.

Repair or Replace a Bar Fridge?

A bar fridge is cheap to replace, so the repair has to make sense. As a rule of thumb, repair it if the fault is a seal, fan, thermostat or start component and the fridge is under about 8 to 10 years old. Think about replacing it if the compressor has failed or the sealed system has leaked, and the repair costs more than about half the price of a new one.

We give you honest advice on the spot. If a repair is not worth it, we say so before you spend anything, even though it means no job for us.

Quick Checks Before You Book

  • Check the power point and the circuit breaker, and confirm the fridge is switched on.
  • Set the thermostat to a middle setting and wait a few hours.
  • Leave a gap of at least 10 cm around the sides and back so the fridge can breathe.
  • Clean the dusty condenser coil or grille at the back or underneath.
  • Check the door seal closes on a sheet of paper, and write down the brand and model.

Bar Fridge Repair FAQs

How much does bar fridge repair cost in Blacktown?

Our call-out fee starts from $69 and it covers the visit and the diagnosis. The repair price depends on the fault and the part, so you get an upfront quote before any work starts and you approve it first.

Is it worth repairing a bar fridge or mini fridge?

It depends on the fault and the age. A seal, thermostat, fan or start component is usually worth fixing. A failed compressor or a sealed system leak on a small, older bar fridge often costs more than the fridge is worth, and we tell you that before you spend anything.

Why is my bar fridge not cold?

Common causes are a dirty or blocked condenser, a failed fan, a faulty thermostat, a worn door seal, a compressor start component fault or a gas leak. Poor ventilation around the fridge, or a very warm room, makes it worse.

Why is my mini fridge freezing the food or making ice?

Food freezing usually points to a thermostat set too cold or a faulty thermostat. Ice build-up often comes from a worn door seal, a door left ajar or a defrost fault.

Why is my bar fridge noisy?

Rattling is often a loose panel or a fan blade, and a loud hum or clicking can come from the compressor or its start relay. A fridge that is not level or touching a wall can also be loud. We find the source before replacing anything.

Can you repair glass door bar fridges and drinks fridges?

Yes. We repair glass door bar fridges, under-bench drinks fridges, bottle coolers and compact fridges, at homes and in venues. For busy commercial display cabinets, see our display fridge page.

Do you repair camping and 12V fridges?

Yes. We repair most portable compressor fridges and 12V camping fridges. Common faults are power or voltage cut-out problems, wiring and plug issues, fans and thermostats.

Can you regas a bar fridge?

Yes, with R134a or R600a gas, after finding and repairing the leak. Small bar fridges are often not worth a sealed-system repair, and we will tell you before any work starts.

Do you come to my home to repair a bar fridge?

Yes. We come to your home or venue, diagnose the fridge on site and repair it where we can. Tell us the brand, the model and what the fridge is doing when you book.

Do you offer a warranty on bar fridge repairs?

Yes. Every repair carries a warranty of up to 12 months on parts and labour.
