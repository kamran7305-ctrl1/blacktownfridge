Signs Your Westinghouse Fridge Needs Repair

Most faults give you some warning. Get in touch if you notice any of these:

Fridge Section Is Warm

The freezer is cold but milk and leftovers are not.

Ice at the Back Wall

Frost or a solid sheet of ice behind the back panel.

Water on the Floor

Water pooling inside the fridge or under the crisper drawers.

Loud Noises

Buzzing, clicking, or a fan that rattles or squeals.

Runs All the Time

The compressor never switches off but the fridge stays warm.

Ice or Water Dispenser Stopped

No ice, slow water, or a dispenser that leaks.

Door Will Not Seal

A worn or torn seal, or a door that hangs open.

Fridge Is Dead

No lights and no cooling, or it trips the power.

Find Your Westinghouse Model Number

The model number is on the data plate, usually inside the fridge section on a side wall or near the top. Send it to us when you book. It tells us the layout, the age of the fridge and which parts to bring, which saves a second visit.

If you cannot find the plate, tell us the type of fridge, roughly how old it is and what it is doing, and we will work from there.

Quick Checks Before You Book

  • Check the power point and the circuit breaker, and confirm the fridge is switched on.
  • Set the fridge to 3 to 5 degrees C and the freezer to -18 degrees C.
  • Check the door seal closes on a sheet of paper and nothing blocks the vents.
  • Pull the fridge a few centimetres off the wall so the back can breathe.
  • Write down the model number and what the fridge is doing.

Food Safety While Your Fridge Is Down

Keep the doors shut. Cold food that has stayed above 5 degrees C for under 2 hours can go back in the fridge or be used. Between 2 and 4 hours, use it straight away. Over 4 hours, throw it out. When in doubt, throw it out. The NSW Food Authority has full guidance on food that has been left out of refrigeration.

Westinghouse Fridge Repair FAQs

How much does Westinghouse fridge repair cost in Blacktown?

Our call-out fee starts from $69 and it covers the visit and the diagnosis. The repair price depends on the fault, the part and the model, so you get an upfront quote before any work starts and you approve it first.

Are you a Westinghouse service centre?

No. We are an independent local fridge repair business in Blacktown. Our technicians are fully trained and licensed and repair Westinghouse fridges and freezers, but we are not a factory service centre. If your fridge is still under the manufacturer's warranty, check the warranty terms first.

Can you repair my Westinghouse fridge on the same day?

We offer same-day service where available. Book online early in the day and tell us the model number, the fault and your suburb. If a part has to be ordered, we return once it arrives.

Why is my Westinghouse fridge not cooling but the freezer is cold?

This usually points to an air flow or defrost problem, such as an iced-up evaporator, a failed fan or a faulty damper or sensor. Cold air is not reaching the fridge section. A technician needs to test it, because the symptom is the same for several different faults.

Why is my Westinghouse freezer full of ice?

Heavy frost usually comes from a defrost fault, a worn door seal or a door that does not close fully. Scraping the ice off is a short-term fix only, because the cause is still there.

Why is my Westinghouse fridge leaking water?

The common causes are a blocked or frozen defrost drain, a split water line to the ice and water dispenser, or a failed inlet valve. Switch off the water supply if the leak is from the dispenser line and book a repair.

Can you repair Westinghouse French door, side by side and bottom mount fridges?

Yes. We repair top mount, bottom mount, side by side and French door models. Send us the model number from the data plate when you book, so we can bring the right parts.

What should I do if my Westinghouse fridge stops cooling?

Keep the doors shut, check the power point and the circuit breaker, and move essential food to an esky with ice if it will be hours before we arrive. Do not keep opening the doors to check the temperature.

Is it worth repairing an older Westinghouse fridge?

Usually, if the fridge is under about 10 years old and the repair costs less than half the price of a new one. We tell you honestly if a repair does not make sense, for example a failed compressor on an old unit.

Do you offer a warranty on Westinghouse repairs?

Yes. Every repair carries a warranty of up to 12 months on parts and labour.
