What To Do While You Wait for the Technician

  1. 1
    Keep the doors closed. A closed fridge holds its cold for a few hours. Every opening lets it out.
  2. 2
    Do not keep switching it off and on. Repeated restarts can damage the compressor.
  3. 3
    Check the basics. Is the power on at the wall, has the safety switch tripped, is the temperature dial where it should be?
  4. 4
    Move the food that matters most. Meat, seafood, dairy, cooked leftovers, baby milk and medication go first.
  5. 5
    Use ice. Put the high-risk food in an esky or cooler with ice or frozen packs.
  6. 6
    Write down the brand, model number and any fault code on the display. You will find the model number on a sticker inside the door or on the back.
  7. 7
    Keep the freezer shut. A full freezer can stay frozen for a day or longer if it is not opened.

How long can food stay out of the fridge?

As a general food safety guide, cold food that has been between 5°C and 60°C for less than 2 hours can go back in the fridge or be used. Between 2 and 4 hours, use it straight away. After 4 hours, throw it out. When in doubt, throw it out. For official advice, see the NSW Food Authority.

Emergency Freezer Repair

A failing freezer is a slow-motion emergency. You may not notice until the ice cream goes soft. We do emergency freezer repair on upright freezers, chest freezers, fridge-freezers and commercial freezers. Common causes include a failed evaporator fan, a defrost fault, a worn door seal, a blocked drain or a refrigerant leak. If your freezer has started to thaw, keep it shut, do not refreeze anything that has fully defrosted, and call us.

Emergency Fridge Repair Cost in Blacktown

We would rather tell you the price than make you guess. The call-out fee is $69 and the phone quote is free. The repair price depends on the fault, and we confirm it with you on site before we start. A blocked drain or a door seal is a small job. A compressor or a sealed-system leak is a bigger one. If a repair is not worth doing because the fridge is old and the fault is major, we will say so and help you work out whether to replace it.

Things that affect the final price:

  • The part that has failed
  • The age and model of the fridge
  • Whether refrigerant is needed
  • How easy it is to get at the fault

Is it worth repairing, or should you replace it?

If the fridge is under about 10 years old and the fault is a fan, seal, thermostat, sensor or defrost part, repair usually makes sense. If it is old and has a failed compressor or a leaking sealed system, replacement is often the better call. Either way, an emergency repair can keep your food safe while you decide.
