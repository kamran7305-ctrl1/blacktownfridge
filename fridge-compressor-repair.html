Signs Your Fridge Compressor Is Failing

The compressor pumps refrigerant around the fridge. Get in touch if you notice any of these:

Fridge and Freezer Both Warm

The compressor is not moving gas, or not starting.

Clicks Then Stops

The relay or overload cuts out as the compressor tries to start.

Very Hot Compressor

Hot to touch and running without a break.

Loud Hum or Rattle

A deep hum, buzzing or knocking from the back.

Runs Non-Stop

The compressor never cycles off.

Trips the Power

The fridge trips the breaker when it starts.

Oily Residue

Oil near a pipe or joint can point to a leak.

Slow Decline

Cooling getting worse over weeks.

Fridge Compressor Repair and Replacement

Diagnosis first

We test the supply, the start components, the compressor windings and the pressure in the system before recommending a replacement.

What a replacement involves

The new compressor is fitted, the system is leak tested and vacuumed, then recharged with the correct gas by weight. R134a and R600a systems are both covered.

Why it costs more than other repairs

It is sealed-system work: parts, refrigerant handling and time. That is why we quote it clearly before we start, and why the repair-or-replace decision matters.

Repair or Replace the Fridge?

As a rule of thumb, repair a fridge that is under about 10 years old when the whole repair costs less than half the price of a comparable new fridge. Consider replacing it if the cabinet is rusted, the seals are worn or the fridge has had earlier gas leaks.

We give you honest advice on the spot. Bar fridges and old chest freezers are often not worth a compressor repair. See bar fridge repair and freezer repair.

Quick Checks Before You Book

  • Check the power point and the circuit breaker, and confirm the fridge is switched on.
  • Pull the fridge a few centimetres off the wall and listen for the compressor starting.
  • Clean dust from the condenser coil if you can reach it safely.
  • Do not keep switching the fridge on and off. Wait at least 5 minutes between attempts.
  • Write down the brand, the model number and what the compressor is doing.

Fridge Compressor Repair FAQs

How much does fridge compressor repair cost in Blacktown?

Our call-out fee starts from $69 and it covers the visit and the diagnosis. A compressor job is one of the bigger fridge repairs, so you get an upfront quote after diagnosis and you approve it before any work starts. To confirm pricing for your fridge, contact us through the booking form or email.

How do I know if my fridge compressor has failed?

Typical signs are a fridge and freezer that are both warm, a compressor that never starts, clicks or hums and then stops, or a compressor that is very hot and runs non-stop. The cause is often a cheaper start component or fan, so a technician tests before calling it a compressor fault.

Can the problem be the start relay instead of the compressor?

Yes. A failed start relay, overload or capacitor stops a healthy compressor from starting, and it is much cheaper to fix. We test these first.

Is it worth replacing a fridge compressor?

Sometimes. If the fridge is under about 10 years old and the rest of it is in good condition, replacing the compressor can be worthwhile. On an old or rusted fridge, a compressor replacement often costs more than half the price of a new one, and we will tell you so.

What is the difference between a regas and a compressor repair?

A regas deals with refrigerant that has leaked out. A compressor repair deals with the pump that circulates the gas. If a fridge is low on gas, we find and repair the leak first. If the compressor has failed, it is a larger sealed-system repair.

Why is my fridge compressor very hot or running all the time?

It can be a dirty condenser, a failed fan, a weak door seal, low refrigerant or a worn compressor. A hot compressor that never stops is working hard for a reason, and we find the cause before replacing anything.

Why does my fridge click and then stop?

Clicking and stopping usually means the start relay or overload is cutting out because the compressor is struggling to start, or the compressor is seized. It needs testing, as running it repeatedly can do more damage.

Do you repair compressors on commercial fridges and coolrooms?

Yes. We repair and replace compressors and condensing units on commercial fridges, freezers, display fridges, coolrooms and freezer rooms.

Do you repair inverter and linear compressor fridges?

We repair many modern household fridges, including those with inverter compressors. The fault is often in the control board or sensors rather than the compressor itself, so we test first.

Do you offer a warranty on compressor repairs?

Yes. Every repair carries a warranty of up to 12 months on parts and labour.
