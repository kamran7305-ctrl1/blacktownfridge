Signs Your Fisher & Paykel Fridge Needs Repair

Most faults give you some warning. Get in touch if you notice any of these:

Fridge Section Is Warm

Milk and leftovers feel warm while the freezer stays cold.

Ice Build-Up

Frost on the back wall of the freezer or in the fridge section.

Beeping or Flashing Lights

An alarm or display that will not clear after the door is closed.

Water Leaking

Water pooling inside the fridge or on the kitchen floor.

Loud Noises

Buzzing, clicking or a fan that rattles or squeals.

Ice Maker Stopped

No ice, small ice, or a dispenser that will not pour.

Door Will Not Seal

A worn seal, a door that hangs open or a light that stays on.

Compressor Runs Constantly

The fridge never reaches a stable temperature.

Beeping, Flashing Lights and Display Alerts

What to note before you book

A flashing light or alert shows which part of the fridge detected a problem, not which part has failed. A door left open, a temperature that drifted after a power cut and a failed sensor can all look similar. Before you book, write down what the display shows, whether the alarm stops when the door closes, and when it started.

Why a reset is not a repair

Switching the fridge off at the wall for a few minutes can clear a temporary alert. If the alert comes back, the fault is still there. We test the sensors, fans and control board before replacing anything, so you pay for the part that is actually faulty.

Quick Checks Before You Book

  • Check the power point and the circuit breaker, and confirm the fridge is switched on.
  • Set the fridge to 3 to 5 degrees C and the freezer to -18 degrees C.
  • Check the door seal closes on a sheet of paper and nothing blocks the vents.
  • Pull the fridge a few centimetres off the wall so the back can breathe.
  • Write down the model number from the data plate and any alert on the display.

Food Safety While Your Fridge Is Down

Keep the doors shut. Cold food that has stayed above 5 degrees C for under 2 hours can go back in the fridge or be used. Between 2 and 4 hours, use it straight away. Over 4 hours, throw it out. When in doubt, throw it out. The NSW Food Authority has full guidance on food that has been left out of refrigeration.

Fisher & Paykel Fridge Repair FAQs

How much does a Fisher & Paykel fridge repair cost in Blacktown?

Our call-out fee starts from $69 and it covers the visit and the diagnosis. The repair price depends on the fault and the part, so we give you an upfront quote after diagnosis and you approve it before any work starts.

Are you a Fisher & Paykel service centre?

No. We are an independent local fridge repair business in Blacktown. We repair Fisher & Paykel fridges and freezers with fully trained and licensed technicians, but we are not a factory service centre. If your fridge is still under Fisher & Paykel's manufacturer warranty, check the warranty terms first.

Can you repair my Fisher & Paykel fridge the same day?

We offer same-day service where available. Book online early in the day and tell us the model, the fault and your suburb. If a part has to be ordered, we return once it arrives.

Why is my Fisher & Paykel fridge not cooling but the freezer is cold?

This usually points to an air flow or defrost problem, such as an iced-up evaporator, a failed fan or a faulty damper or sensor. Cold air is not reaching the fridge section. A technician needs to test it, because the visible symptom is the same for several different faults.

What do the flashing lights or beeps on my Fisher & Paykel fridge mean?

They usually mean the fridge has detected a fault, a door has been left open or the temperature has gone out of range. Write down what the display shows and how the lights flash, then tell us when you book. We test the system to find the part that is actually at fault before anything is replaced.

Do you repair Fisher & Paykel French door and Quad Door fridges?

Yes. We repair French door, bottom mount, top mount, side by side and Quad Door models, as well as integrated and Column fridges and freezers. Send us the model number from the data plate when you book so we can bring the right parts.

Can you fix a Fisher & Paykel ice and water dispenser?

Often yes. Common faults are a blocked or frozen water line, a failed inlet valve, a clogged filter or an ice maker that has stopped harvesting. We check the water supply and the parts in turn.

Is it worth repairing an older Fisher & Paykel fridge?

Usually, if the fridge is under about 10 years old and the repair costs less than half the price of a new one. We tell you honestly if a repair does not make sense, for example a failed compressor on an old unit.

What should I do if my Fisher & Paykel fridge stops cooling?

Keep the doors shut, check the power point and circuit breaker, and move essential food to an esky with ice if it will be hours before we arrive. Do not keep opening the doors to check the temperature.

Do you offer a warranty on Fisher & Paykel repairs?

Yes. Every repair carries a warranty of up to 12 months on parts and labour.
