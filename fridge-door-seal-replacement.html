Signs Your Fridge Door Seal Is Worn

A failing seal rarely announces itself. Check for these signs:

Tears or Cracks

Splits in the rubber, especially at the corners.

Gaps Around the Door

You can see light or feel cold air at the edge.

Ice and Condensation

Frost near the door, or water collecting in the fridge.

Mould in the Folds

Black or green marks in the bellows of the seal.

Door Does Not Close

The door springs open or stays slightly ajar.

Fridge Runs Constantly

The compressor works harder to keep up.

Rising Power Bills

Electricity use creeping up with no other cause.

Paper Slides Out Easily

A sheet closed in the door pulls out with no grip.

If the fridge is also warm, see fridge not cooling.

How a Worn Seal Costs You Money

It makes the compressor work harder

When warm air leaks in, the fridge runs for longer to keep the temperature down. That wears the compressor and raises the power bill.

It causes ice and a damp fridge

Moist air getting into the cold space freezes on the coil and the freezer wall. Ice blocks air flow, and the fridge section can warm up as a result.

It is a food safety risk

A fridge that cannot hold 5 degrees C or below puts food at risk. A seal replacement is a small job compared with replacing stock or the fridge.

Quick Checks Before You Book

  • Close the door on a sheet of paper in several places and note where it slides out.
  • Wipe the seal with warm soapy water and dry it. Dirt and spills can stop it gripping.
  • Check that the fridge is level and the door is not hitting a wall or cupboard.
  • Check the shelves and bottles are not stopping the door from closing.
  • Write down the brand, model number and the size of the door.

Fridge Door Seal Replacement FAQs

How much does fridge door seal replacement cost in Blacktown?

Our call-out fee starts from $69 and it covers the visit and the diagnosis. The price of the seal depends on the model and size, so you get an upfront quote before any work starts and you approve it first.

How do I know my fridge door seal needs replacing?

Look for tears, gaps, hardened or flattened rubber, mould in the folds, a door that does not close on its own, or ice and condensation near the door. A simple test is to close the door on a sheet of paper. If it pulls out with no resistance, the seal is not gripping.

Can a fridge door seal be repaired instead of replaced?

Small gaps are sometimes fixed by warming and reshaping the seal or adjusting the door, but a torn, hardened or mouldy seal needs replacing. We tell you which one it is after checking the door.

Is a faulty door seal the cause of my fridge running all the time?

It can be. A worn seal lets warm air in, so the compressor runs constantly, the freezer frosts up and power use climbs. We also check the fan, defrost system and thermostat, because the symptoms overlap.

Can you replace a fridge seal at my home?

Yes. We come to you, check the seal and the door, and fit the replacement on site. If the seal for your model is not on the van, we order the correct one and return to fit it.

Do you replace commercial fridge and coolroom door seals?

Yes. We replace seals on commercial fridges, freezers, display fridges, coolroom doors and freezer room doors, including strip curtains and door heaters. Tell us the brand, the model and the size when you book.

Which fridge brands can you replace door seals on?

We replace seals on most household and commercial brands, including Samsung, LG, Westinghouse, Electrolux and Fisher & Paykel, and commercial brands such as Skope, Bromic, Williams, Husky and True. Send us the model number from the data plate.

How long does a door seal replacement take?

Fitting a seal usually takes under an hour once we have the correct part. The seal has to be warmed and seated properly so it grips, which is why we do not just press it on.

Can I do a door seal replacement myself?

Some seals are push-in and some are screwed behind the gasket. The wrong part or a poor fit leaves gaps that cost more in power and food. Booking a technician is usually the safer choice, and we can check whether the seal is the real fault.

Do you offer a warranty on seal replacement?

Yes. Every repair carries a warranty of up to 12 months on parts and labour.
